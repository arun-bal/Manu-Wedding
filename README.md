## ✨ Key Interactive Features

1. **Wax Seal Opening & Background Music**:
   - Royal Navy & Gold wax seal stamped with `M & M` monogram.
   - Tap to open with 3D animation, starting the ambient wedding music.
2. **French Blue & Ivory Botanical Aesthetic**:
   - Soft warm linen paper background, delicate blue hydrangeas, sage foliage, and classic royal navy calligraphy.
3. **Dual-Event Scratch-to-Reveal**:
   - Gold foil scratch card unveils both the Wedding (Cheruvathur) and Reception (Vadakara) with celebratory blue hydrangea and gold petal showers.
   - Includes one-tap "Add to Google Calendar" and Apple/iCal buttons for both events.
4. **Interactive Well Wishes Wall (Guestbook)**:
   - Quick wedding emoji toolbar (💍, 🌸, 🥂, ❤️, 💐, 🕊️, 🎉, 🧿, ✨, 💙).
   - Client-side auto-compressed photo attachments (< 80KB) with lightbox zoom.
   - Animated celebratory GIF stickers.
   - Interactive heart/like reactions on posted wishes.
5. **Couples Photos & Lightbox**:
   - High-resolution couple portrait and ring exchange ceremony photos with full-screen lightbox preview.
6. **Dual Venue Cards with Google Maps**:
   - Directions and one-tap map buttons for both Cheruvathur and Vadakara.
7. **WhatsApp Sharing**:
   - Floating WhatsApp sharing button with customized message preview.
